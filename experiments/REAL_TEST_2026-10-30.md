## Findings and fixes

1. Domain collisions: CRM/software development and industrial automation titles inflated Farhan's ranking; site/database reliability titles inflated Sana's. Added a priority-only cap for specific mismatched title families. This changes ranking, never eligibility.
   In the fixed top 30, the targeted mismatches dropped from 13 to 0 for Farhan and 12 to 0 for Sana. These are counts under the new title patterns on this same dataset, not an independently labeled precision score. Sana's 12 included 11 software reliability records plus one Energy Data Scientist.
2. False exclusion: GitLab's regional salary disclosure included “United States only.” The old rule treated that as hiring geography. Salary/compensation sentences are now excluded from this narrow geography scan; explicit separate hiring restrictions remain detectable. Farhan REJECT fell by 97 across the dataset. Those 97 are unsupported rejection reasons removed, not 97 newly eligible jobs.
3. Missing input: a nonexistent/empty board directory silently produced a successful zero-record report. The trial now fails clearly. A valid board with zero jobs remains allowed.

## Limits and safety

No live URLs, vacancy status, salary thresholds, work authorization, exact seniority, certifications or application history were independently verified. No candidate CV/private profile was read, so exact candidate fit cannot be scored. Long descriptions are treated as full JDs using a word-count heuristic only.
Deduplication is by collector key, with URL fallback within a board; 818 unique keys does not establish 818 unique underlying vacancies. Regional copies can crowd the ranking.
The narrow regex rules can miss restrictions and overgeneralize titles. Salary-sentence filtering intentionally leaves ambiguous mixed statements on HOLD rather than rejecting.
Sana's all-HOLD result is an unimplemented qualification layer, not a successful recruiter filter. No defensible overall false-positive/false-negative rate can be stated without independent labels.
No paid APIs, paid services, applications, messages, ledger writes, scheduling changes or production changes were used. Only experiments/ changes are intended for the experiment branch. No merge.
